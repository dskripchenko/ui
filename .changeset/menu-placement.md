UidMenu: a `placement` prop (`'top' | 'top-start' | 'top-end' | 'bottom' | 'bottom-start' | 'bottom-end' | 'left' | 'right'`, default `'bottom-start'`) — a row menu at the right edge of a table opens with `'bottom-end'` and aligns to the trigger's right edge. An open menu follows a changed placement. The `UidMenuProps` type is exported.
