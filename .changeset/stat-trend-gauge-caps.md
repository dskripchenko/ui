UidStat: `trendPlacement` (`inline` | `below`). `below` puts the trend on its own line under the value, so a row of cards stays uniform whatever the length of each value; `inline` (default) keeps the previous behaviour.

UidGauge: with `ranges`, the track's round caps no longer peek out as faint dots past the zones' square ends.
