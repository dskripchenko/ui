Navigation for long lists. UidSidebarSection — a folding section with a summary and head buttons; in a collapsed sidebar an icon with its count that opens the section as a flyout. UidSidebarItem — a dense 28px row with a meta (time), a danger "@N" for mentions, a neutral count, bold for new, dimmed for muted, a suffix tag and actions that take the place of the meta on hover and focus. UidSidebarMore — "13 more unread" and "All channels · 134". UidSidebarJump — the "Go to… ⌘K" field on top. UidDataList — a catalogue: search with "/", dropdown filters, chips with counts, columns, groups, empty and loading.
