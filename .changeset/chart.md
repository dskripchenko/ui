UidChart: a general SVG chart — line, area, bar (grouped or stacked) and dot series over a shared category axis, x and y reference lines, a fixed colour-blind-safe palette (`--uid-chart-1` … `--uid-chart-6`, `--uid-chart-neutral`) with light and dark values, semantic tones, a tooltip on hover and from the keyboard, a legend, a visually hidden data table and an empty state. No dependencies. Also exports the `niceTicks(min, max, count)` helper and the `chart` locale strings (en, ru).
