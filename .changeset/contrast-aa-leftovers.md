The rest of AA contrast. Accent-coloured text (links, active tabs, sidebar items, selected options and dates, chips, sort headers, anchors) uses `--uid-accent-text`, through the new alias `--uid-color-primary-text`: 5.47:1 on white instead of 2.49. Avatar fills are the 700 steps of the same eight hues, so white initials reach 5.02:1 or more; the name still picks the same hue. A checked checkbox and radio, a switch that is on and the audio play button use `--uid-accent-solid`, so they stand out from the surface at 3:1 or more (WCAG 1.4.11).
